# Underwriting Guide (fictional)

> Demo content for a fictional insurance carrier ("Northwind"). Not real
> underwriting, rating, regulatory, or legal advice. The app recommends a
> workflow status for a human underwriter; it never binds coverage or prices
> premium.

This guide defines the set of businesses the carrier wants to insure. Use it
to decide whether a submission is eligible, needs a condition, needs more
information, needs a senior underwriter, or should be declined.

## Eligible

- Common small-business types: shops, offices, restaurants, fitness studios,
  self-storage, light workshops, **owner-occupied or well-managed**.
- Buildings in sound condition. Property value up to **$25M** per location.

## Needs a condition (quote-with-conditions)

- An older building feature in otherwise-good shape (e.g. an aging roof) →
  attach a condition such as an inspection or a repair warranty.
- A business in a higher-hazard area (heavy wind, hail, or wildfire region) →
  attach a higher deductible for that hazard.
- A restaurant that cooks → require an up-to-date kitchen fire-safety
  inspection.

## Refer to a senior underwriter (refer)

- Property value over **$25M**.
- Any single past claim over **$100k**, or an **open** claim of any size that
  isn't yet resolved.
- A **repeat** claim of the same cause (e.g. two kitchen fires), a sign of an
  uncorrected hazard.
- Considering an exception to write something this guide lists as ineligible.

## Ineligible (decline)

- **Vacant or unoccupied** buildings.
- Buildings with known structural problems or open code violations.
- Prohibited business types: fireworks, cannabis growing, oil & gas extraction,
  explosives.

## Claims history, in short

- No claims, or one small old claim → fine.
- Frequent claims (3+ in the last 3 years) → `needs-review`.
- A large (> $100k) or open claim, or a repeat cause → `refer`.
